Another possibility is temporary-storage lifetime across the no-grad forward, differentiable forward and backward. Views and saved tensors can retain backing storage beyond the apparent lifetime of a local variable, and repeated allocation of differently padded buffers could interact with allocator reuse and autograd bookkeeping. This could make whole-call latency sensitive to the ownership and lifetime of buffers, even when an individual indexing operation looks inexpensive. With eight local experts and sixteen source groups, equal total row counts need not imply equal loop work: fragmentation, short chunks and zero-length groups can alter how much useful work each program performs.
